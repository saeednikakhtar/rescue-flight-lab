Detection & recovery rules

Warning below 200 m. The acceleration entry path below 100 m requires down acceleration above 5 m/s², confirmed descent above 0.5 m/s for at least 1 s with 2 m height loss, then a continuous 0.2 s entry dwell. Additional low-height safeguard: confirmed sink above 5 m/s (or above 2 m/s below 30 m) with less than 6 s to the Home datum, sustained for 0.2 s without verified braking clearance. This extends the acceleration rule. Rotation alone never captures control.

Stable for 2 s: 200 ± 5 m, |vertical speed| ≤ 1 m/s, |roll / pitch| ≤ 10°, rotation ≤ 15°/s and airspeed ≥ 10 m/s. The pilot then has 5 s to take control.

Existing TECS manages speed and height. During recovery, height or equivalent energy must improve; throttle position alone is not evidence of a climb. Failure guards can request the parachute.

Entry thresholds use height above Home. Ground clearance is displayed separately.
